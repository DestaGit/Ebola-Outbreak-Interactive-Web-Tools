WHY IS 2014 WEST AFRICA LOWER? · The 2014–2016 outbreak had a lower CFR than most prior outbreaks (39.5% vs 52–88%) largely because it occurred in densely populated urban areas where detection rates were higher — including milder or subclinical infections counted in the denominator.
⚠ STATISTICAL NOTE · The Wilson Score confidence interval is preferred over the Wald interval for proportions near 0 or 1 because it maintains coverage probability even with small samples. For very small n (n<30), interpret CI bounds with extra caution. CFR figures from historical outbreaks are point estimates from WHO Disease Outbreak News reports and may differ from later revised figures.
