What If? Simulator
Counterfactual epidemic curves. Three live sliders reshape the outbreak in real time — intervention timing, effectiveness, and baseline spread rate.

District Profiler
Filterable, sortable explorer of 15 sub-national districts. Click any card to open a detailed side panel with severity rating and CFR comparison.

CFR Calculator
Enter any case/death count. Computes CFR with 95% Wilson Score CI and benchmarks it against 8 historical Ebola outbreaks from 1976–2022.
Chart.jsWilson Score CI

Deep dive into 886 healthcare worker infections. HCW surge preceded the general population peak by 3 weeks. Country breakdown and context.
